# Jurisdiction and Market Assumptions for Legal Review

**Engineering status:** no public-beta/commercial launch jurisdiction has been legally approved.

The current engineering plan assumes source distribution can be public and global, while optional AI/provider egress is user-selected and may send data to third parties in other jurisdictions. The project does not claim that one license/FTO/privacy conclusion is valid worldwide.

Before public beta or commercial use, counsel must be given the intended launch countries/regions, distribution channels, monetization model, hosted-service scope if any, AI/provider integrations, trademark/brand use, and the preview/network/storage/runtime topology.

Until that review is recorded:

- P16-01 final project license remains open.
- P16-05 FTO review remains open.
- engineering docs must not make universal patent/FTO or regulatory-compliance claims.
- commercial launch/promotion must remain blocked by release governance.

This record exists so counsel receives explicit assumptions rather than having engineering prose silently imply a universal legal conclusion.
