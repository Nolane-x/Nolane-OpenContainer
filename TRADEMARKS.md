# OpenContainer Name and Trademark Policy

This policy is separate from any source-code license.

No statement in the repository claims that "OpenContainer", Nolane names, logos, icons or visual marks are registered trademarks. No source-code license is currently finalized, and no trademark rights are granted by implication.

Descriptive references to the project may identify compatibility, origin or interoperability when they do not imply sponsorship or official status. Forks and redistributed builds should use clear attribution and must not present themselves as an official Nolane/OpenContainer release unless authorized by the project owner.

Logos and brand assets, if added, require separate permission unless an accompanying file grants broader rights. This policy may be revised before public commercial launch after legal review.
